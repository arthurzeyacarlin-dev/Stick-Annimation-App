The Stick Figure Workspace is the animation environment used for stick figure animation projects.

Its purpose is to let users animate stick figures quickly and professionally.

The workspace is not meant to be a copy of the drawing workspace. Its focus is figure motion, pose editing, and stick-based animation structure.

The central stage is where the user interacts with stick figures.

The right-side tools are focused on figure creation, figure editing, pose adjustment, and animation controls.

The AI system in this workspace should help accelerate motion-related animation tasks.

The bottom timeline controls frame timing, keyframes, tween timing, and playback structure.

The workspace should feel simple, fast, and capable of serious stick figure animation.
