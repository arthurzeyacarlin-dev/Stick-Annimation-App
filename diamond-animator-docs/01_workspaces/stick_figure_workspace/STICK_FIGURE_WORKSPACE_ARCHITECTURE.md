The Stick Figure Workspace Architecture defines the structure of the stick animation environment inside Diamond Animator.

This workspace is focused on creating and animating stick figures.

The layout contains four major areas.

The top command bar contains project-level controls.

The center stage is where stick figures are placed, posed, and animated.

The right-side panel contains figure-related tools, motion controls, and AI animation support.

The bottom timeline controls timing and animation structure.

This workspace should support a hybrid design direction.

It should be simple enough for easy stick animation use, but strong enough to grow into more advanced workflows later.

The workspace should feel clean, understandable, and serious.
