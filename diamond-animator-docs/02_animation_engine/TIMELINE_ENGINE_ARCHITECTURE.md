The Timeline System is responsible for displaying and managing animation time inside the workspace.

The timeline displays animation frames horizontally and animation layers vertically.

Users can extend the timeline vertically to reveal additional animation lanes.

When many layers are present, the timeline supports vertical scrolling so that users can navigate complex animation structures.

The timeline also supports sound events.

Sound effects are placed on a dedicated sound lane that appears near the bottom of the timeline.

Sound events are not tied to a specific drawing layer. Instead they apply to the animation as a whole.

Each sound event appears at a specific frame location and includes a sound name generated by the AI system or assigned by the user.

Users can click the sound icon on the sound event to adjust properties such as volume.

The timeline must remain readable even when the project becomes large.

Scrollbars appear only when the timeline contains more lanes than can be displayed at once.
