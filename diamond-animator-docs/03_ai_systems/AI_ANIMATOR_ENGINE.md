The AI Animator Engine is the main in-workspace AI productivity engine of Diamond Animator.

Its purpose is to help the user animate faster while they are actively working inside a workspace.

The AI Animator Engine may help with tasks such as frame generation, timing assistance, motion support, and rough workflow acceleration.

The engine should feel like a strong helper built into the workspace.

It should never take over the user’s authorship.

Its role is to make animation faster, cleaner, and easier to continue.

The AI Animator Engine must remain useful, understandable, and trustworthy.

The AI Assistant System is the general guidance AI for Diamond Animator.

This system is mainly used to help users understand the app, find the right tools, and know what to do next.

It is especially important on the main screen and in situations where the user needs help navigating the product.

The user may ask questions such as how to export, how to start a project, or which workspace to choose.

The assistant should answer clearly and should be able to guide the user toward the correct tool or screen.

The AI Assistant should feel like a helpful built-in guide, not just a random chatbot.

Its purpose is to reduce confusion and help users move forward quickly.
