The AI Motion Interpolation system helps support motion between animation states.

Its job is to assist with movement progression and smoother transition behavior between defined points in animation.

This system may be especially useful when the user wants support for motion flow and timing between key points.

The interpolation system should remain understandable and should not behave like an uncontrollable black box.

Its purpose is to support animation speed and smoother workflow while keeping the creator in control.
