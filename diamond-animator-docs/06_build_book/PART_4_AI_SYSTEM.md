Part 4 — AI Systems

AI systems are integrated into Diamond Animator to assist users during the animation process.

These systems are designed to improve productivity rather than replace the animator.

The AI systems can assist with generating animation frames, creating sound effects, improving motion timing, and polishing animation results.

When AI systems generate frames, the generated frames appear highlighted so that users can identify them easily.

This highlight gradually fades as the frames integrate into the normal animation timeline.

The AI systems provide feedback to the user while performing tasks. Progress indicators show when the AI is generating frames or performing other actions.

The user interacts with the AI through a dedicated AI assistant panel within the workspace.

The assistant allows the user to request tasks, adjust reasoning levels, and select different types of AI operations.

This system allows creators to accelerate the animation process while remaining in control of their work.
