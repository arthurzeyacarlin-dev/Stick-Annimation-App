# Diamond Animator: monthly prices, our costs, and profit

Researched 2026-10-08 (web only; nothing signed up for or bought). Follows `2026-10-08-website-pricing-research.md`.

How to read the labels:
- **Official** means I read the company's own pricing page today.
- **Secondary** means a 2026 review or guide site, because the official page hid prices behind JavaScript or showed Thai baht.
- **Estimate** means my own assumption. The reason is given next to it.

**Check every price on the live site before it goes on our website.**

---

## The short answer (for dad)

1. **What the market charges each month.**
   - Hobby and kid animation apps: **free to $6/mo**, or a one-time purchase of $4–$60.
   - Pro animation software: **$23–$143/mo**.
   - AI animation and AI video tools: **$8–$45/mo** for the plans creators buy. Business tools charge **$58–$199/mo**.
2. **Recommended plans:**
   - **Starter $5.99/mo**, **Creator $11.99/mo**, **Studio $19.99/mo**.
   - Yearly with 2 months free: **$59 / $119 / $199 a year**.
   - AI credits per month: **300 / 1,200 / 3,000**.
3. **Our fixed costs:**
   - About **$46/mo** in the beta (up to 100 users).
   - About **$71/mo** at 500 users.
   - About **$126/mo** at 2,000 users.
4. **Profit per paying user** (after card fees, AI if every credit is used, and storage):
   - Starter **$4.70/mo (78%)**, Creator **$8.24/mo (69%)**, Studio **$11.45/mo (57%)**.
   - **10 Starter users, or 6 Creator users, pay all the beta costs.**
5. **A free-trial user costs at most about 39¢** (14 days, all 150 AI credits used).

## (b) Our fixed monthly costs at 3 stages

Main assumptions:
- The website runs on Cloudflare (SPEC-0018).
- The app (Next.js) runs on Vercel, with Supabase for projects and the database. **Where the app will be hosted isn't decided yet.**
- The app's sign-in currently stores accounts in a database file on Arthur's computer. A hosted version needs a hosted database, such as Supabase Postgres, which is included in Supabase Pro.
- MP4 export runs in the user's browser (I found no server video encoder in `src/`), so exporting costs us no server time.

Notes:
- **Storage at 200 MB per user:** 100 users = 20 GB, 500 = 100 GB (exactly Supabase Pro's included amount), 2,000 = 400 GB (300 GB over, $6.39).
- **Data transfer** is an **estimate**: each user downloads their own projects about once a month (0.2 GB). At 2,000 users that's 400 GB, which is 150 GB over Pro's allowance, so $13.50.
- **The ~$15 bigger Supabase server at 2,000 users** is an **estimate** I didn't check today. It's only needed if the smallest server gets slow.
- **Cheaper option for later:** Cloudflare R2 stores files for **$0.015/GB-month** with **no data-transfer fees** and 10 GB free (official, developers.cloudflare.com/r2/pricing). Moving project files there would save roughly $20/mo at 2,000 users. That isn't worth doing yet.
- **Not counted:** ads, lawyer, sales tax, refunds and chargebacks, income tax, Arthur/dad's time. Also not counted: **AI costs of the Help Assistant, search and dictation**. They aren't measured as part of the $0.0025 per AI Animator message. Decide whether they use credits.

## (c) Costs for each paying user

**AI (we pay it; credits are included in the price).** We measured about **$0.0025 per AI Animator message** (Luna, average from our runs). Worst case below means the user spends every credit.

| Plan | Credits/mo | Worst-case AI cost/mo |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | 300 | **$0.75** |
| Creator | 1,200 | **$3.00** |
| Studio | 3,000 | **$7.50** |
| Trial (14 days) | 150 | **$0.375** |

Our heaviest real user (~1,080 messages a month) would cost about $2.70, which fits inside Creator.

**Storage (estimate, 200 MB per user):**
- File storage: 0.2 GB × $0.0213 = **$0.004/mo**.
- Data transfer: 0.2 GB × $0.09 = **$0.018/mo**.
- Total ≈ **$0.02 per user per month**. It's $0 while we're inside Supabase Pro's included 100 GB storage and 250 GB data transfer.

**Stripe fee per charge (US cards).**
- The official US page showed me Thai prices, so I used two sources:
  - **2.9% + 30¢** per domestic card, plus 1.5% for international cards: stripe.com/pricing via search, and checkoutpage.com (2026).
  - **Billing 0.7%** of recurring volume: official Stripe page, 2026-10-08.
- Together: **3.6% + 30¢** per charge.

| Price | Fee per charge | Fee per month |
|---|---|---|
| $5.99/mo | $0.52 | $0.52 |
| $11.99/mo | $0.73 | $0.73 |
| $19.99/mo | $1.02 | $1.02 |
| $59/yr | $2.42 | $0.20 |
| $119/yr | $4.58 | $0.38 |
| $199/yr | $7.46 | $0.62 |

- **Yearly plans save fees:** the 30¢ is paid once instead of 12 times.
- If we use Stripe Tax, add about **0.5%** (secondary source, checkoutpage.com 2026).

| AI credits / month | **300** | **1,200** | **3,000** |

| Profit per user/mo, yearly plan | **$3.94 (80%)** | **$6.51 (66%)** | **$8.44 (51%)** |

**Why these prices:**
- **Starter $5.99:** same as FlipaClip Plus and close to Roblox Premium's $4.99. It's an allowance-sized price a kid can ask a parent for, and under the $8–$10 entry price of AI tools (Viggle, Pika, Steve AI).
- **Creator $11.99:** under Krikey Pro and Runway Standard ($15), Animaker Basic ($15 yearly, $27 monthly) and Viggle Live ($15.99). It's the plan for teens and YouTubers who post often.
- **Studio $19.99:** keeps Arthur's $20 top price. It's still far under Pika Creator and Runway Pro ($35) and pro tools (Toon Boom $31–$143, Adobe $22.99).
- **Yearly prices:** $59 and $119 are easy birthday or holiday gifts for parents.
  - If dad wants to push yearly harder, the earlier research's deeper discount ($49 / $99 / $169, ~30% off) still makes money: about $3.14 / $4.91 / $6.03 profit per month in the worst case.
- **Margins are "worst case."** Most users won't spend every credit, so real AI cost and real profit will be better. We'll know after the beta.

**What these numbers mean for each stage.** Example (**estimate**): every user at that stage pays, the mix is 40% Starter / 45% Creator / 15% Studio, everyone is on monthly billing, and AI is worst case.

## (e) What a free trial costs, and how many paying users cover our costs

**One trial user** (14 days, no card, worst case):

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| AI: 150 credits × $0.0025 | $0.375 |
| Storage: 200 MB for half a month, plus data transfer (estimate) | ~$0.01 (or $0 inside included amounts) |
| Email: ~5 trial emails (estimate), at Resend's extra-email price | ~$0.005 (or $0 inside the plan) |
| Stripe | $0 (no card) |
| **Total** | **≈ $0.39 per trial user, worst case** |

- **Example (estimate):** 1,000 trials a month cost at most ~$390. If 10% become paying users, each new customer costs ~$3.90 in trial AI. That's less than one month of Starter profit.
- **Watch out:** trial users are locked out after 14 days (Arthur's decision), but their 200 MB stays stored. That's ~$0.004/mo each until we delete it. Set a rule for how long we keep it (for example 90 days) and put it in the Privacy Policy.

**Break-even:** paying users needed to cover the fixed bill, using worst-case profit per user.

| Stage | Fixed bill/mo | Starter users | Creator users | Studio users |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Beta (≤ 100) | ≈ $46 | **10** | **6** | **5** |
| 500 users | ≈ $71 | **16** | **9** | **7** |
| 2,000 users | ≈ $126 | **27** | **16** | **12** |

Add trial costs on top. For example, 100 trials a month adds ≤ $39, which is about 5 more Creator users.

## (f) Cost of the beta discount and the YouTube code

**Beta: first 50 paying users, 25% off the first year.**
- This brief says 25%. SPEC-0018 and the earlier research say **40%**. **Arthur and dad need to pick one.**
- The cost is money we don't collect, not money we pay out.

| Plan | Lost per user (yearly plan) | Lost per user (monthly, 12 months) | Profit/mo while discounted (worst case) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $14.75 | $17.97 | $2.76 yearly / $3.26 monthly |
| Creator | $29.75 | $35.97 | $4.12 yearly / $5.35 monthly |
| Studio | $49.75 | $59.97 | $4.44 yearly / $6.63 monthly |

- **All 50 spots:** about **$740** (all Starter, yearly) to **$3,000** (all Studio, monthly). If everyone picks Creator, it's about **$1,490–$1,800**.
- **Every plan still makes a profit during the discount.**
- **Stripe setup:**
  - Yearly plans: coupon set to **"once"**.
  - Monthly plans: coupon set to **"repeating, 12 months"**.

**YouTube code: 10% off the first month, one time.**
- Cost per user: **$0.60** (Starter), **$1.20** (Creator), **$2.00** (Studio). 100 Creator sign-ups cost $120.
- **Important:** in Stripe, a "once" coupon on a **yearly** plan takes 10% off the **whole first year**. That's $5.90 / $11.90 / $19.90, 10× more than planned.
  - Either limit the code to the monthly prices, or accept the bigger yearly discount on purpose.
  - Also decide whether it can stack with the beta discount. I suggest no.
