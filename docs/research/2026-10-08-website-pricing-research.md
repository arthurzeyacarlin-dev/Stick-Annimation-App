# Diamond Animator — competitor pricing research and pricing recommendation

Researched 2026-10-08 (web only; nothing signed up for or bought). Each number has its source and date. "Official, fetched 2026-10-08" means I read the company's own page today. Where pages only load with JavaScript, or show local prices because of where I'm browsing from (Cascadeur and Stripe showed Thai baht), I used recent 2026 review sites and marked them. Some sources disagree; those cells say so. **Check every price on the live site before it goes on our website.**

---

## 1. The short answer (for Arthur)

- **Weekly pricing isn't normal for apps like ours.** None of the ~20 web or desktop animation tools I checked sell a weekly subscription. They all sell **monthly + yearly** (or a one-time purchase). Weekly plans mostly show up in phone app stores (example: Plotagon Studio on iPhone, $1.99–$2.99/week). There they make money fast, but almost nobody keeps paying.
- **$10 / $15 / $20 a week is too expensive.** That works out to about **$43 / $65 / $87 a month**, or **$520 / $780 / $1,040 a year**. That's more than Adobe Animate ($264/yr) and close to business tools like Vyond ($699/yr). Teens' parents would say no.
- **Better:** sell monthly and yearly plans at **$5.99 / $11.99 / $19.99 a month**, or **$49 / $99 / $169 a year**. Your $20 top price stays. The entry price comes down so teens and parents can say yes.
- **AI is cheap for us.** Even our heaviest user (~1,080 Luna calls a month) costs about **$2.70 a month**, so every plan can include plenty of credits and still make money.

## 3. What the market tells us

1. **Monthly + yearly is the standard. Weekly isn't.** Every web or desktop tool above sells monthly and/or yearly plans, or a one-time purchase. Yearly discounts run from about **16%** (Krikey, Canva) to **20%** (Runway, Pika), **~30%** (Toon Boom), and up to **40–45%** (Animaker, Powtoon, Vyond).
   - **Weekly only shows up in two places:** phone-app-store subscriptions (Plotagon Studio iOS) and **one-time, non-renewing passes** (Krikey "Week Pass", $6 for 50 credits).
2. **Weekly plans make money fast but people quit fast.** In phone apps, weekly plans grew to **55.5% of in-app subscription revenue in 2025**. But only **5.5%** of weekly subscribers are still paying after about a year, versus **19.9%** for yearly (Adapty 2026 data via airbridge.io, 2026-05-27).
   - RevenueCat's 2026 report: yearly plans earn about **5× more revenue per install** than weekly.
   - Those numbers are all for app-store apps; none of these sources measure web or desktop products.
3. **AI usage is almost always sold as monthly credits that reset, plus top-up packs.** Animaker, Renderforest, Krikey, Vyond, Runway and Pika all work this way. Powtoon gives yearly credits; Steve AI sells minutes.
   - Top-up prices range from cheap (Pika ~$2.67 per 100 credits) to steep (Powtoon ~$33–$39 per 100).
   - Some tools let extra credits roll over while plan credits expire (Pika).
4. **Free plans and watermarks are everywhere.** Most tools have a free tier with a watermark or small limits. Commercial use is often locked to a middle or top plan (Animaker Pro, Pika Pro, Powtoon paid). That annoys people: reviewers call Pika's watermarked $8 plan "essentially a higher-credit free tier".
5. **Trials:** 7 days is common (Adobe, Animaker, FlipaClip). 14 days is offered by Voomly and Cascadeur Pro.
   - Longer trials convert better: 17–32-day trials convert at a median of **42.5%**, 5–9-day trials at 37.4%, and under-4-day trials at 25.5% (RevenueCat 2026). So **our 14-day trial is generous and a good choice.**
   - No-card trials get **3–5× more sign-ups** but convert less: about 8–25% versus 30–60% for card-required trials (Appcues, kirro.io, voxbooster 2026 benchmarks).
6. **Teen and hobby tools are cheap:** free to **$6/mo** (FlipaClip), or **$4–$20 one-time** (Stick Nodes, RoughAnimator, Procreate Dreams). **Business explainer tools** charge **$15–$200/mo**.
   - **Diamond Animator fits in the gap:** AI does the animating, which hobby tools can't do, but the output is stick figures, not business videos. So it should cost a bit more than FlipaClip and much less than Vyond or Powtoon.

## 4. Why weekly $10 / $15 / $20 doesn't fit us

| | $10/wk | $15/wk | $20/wk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Per month (×52 ÷ 12) | **$43.33** | **$65.00** | **$86.67** |
| Per year | **$520** | **$780** | **$1,040** |
| Similar to… | More than Adobe Animate ($263.88/yr) and close to Toon Boom Harmony Advanced ($606/yr) | More than Vyond Starter yearly ($699/yr) | Close to Vyond Professional yearly ($1,199/yr) |

- **Too expensive for teens and parents.** FlipaClip is $5.99 a month. Roblox Premium tops out at $19.99 a month.
- **Card fees eat more.** Stripe charges 2.9% + 30¢ per card charge, plus 0.7% for Stripe Billing (US). Weekly means 52 charges a year instead of 12, so the 30¢ is paid 52 times. More charges also means more failed payments and more "what is this charge?" moments for parents, which can lead to chargebacks.
- **Trust.** Weekly auto-renewing plans have a reputation as a "subscription trap" in app stores. That's the opposite of what a kid-friendly brand needs.
- **What to do instead:** monthly + yearly, with yearly as the default. If someone only needs it for a week (say, a school project), the **14-day free trial** covers that. Later we could add a **one-time Project Pass** that doesn't renew, the way Krikey does, if people ask for it.

## 5. Recommendation for Diamond Animator

### Credits and costs (the maths behind the plans)
- **1 AI credit = 1 message to Luna** (a new animation request or an edit like "make it slower"). Measure in the beta how many Luna calls one message really triggers, and adjust if it's more than one.
- Our cost is **$0.0025 per call**, so **100 credits cost us 25¢**.
- A heavy user (~250 calls a week ≈ 1,080 a month) costs us about **$2.70 a month**.
- Plan credits stay **at or under ~38% of the plan price even if a user spends every credit**. Most people use far less.
- Later features (non-stick-figure characters, better models) can cost **more credits per action**. Say so in the AI-credit terms now, so we never have to cut credits later.

Why these numbers:
- **Starter ($5.99)** matches FlipaClip Plus and sits next to Roblox Premium's $4.99. It's an "allowance-sized" price a teen can ask a parent for.
- **Creator ($11.99)** is the plan we want most people on. It's below Krikey Pro ($15), Animaker Basic ($15 yearly) and Canva Pro ($15–$18).
- **Studio ($19.99)** keeps Arthur's $20 top price and stays under the "$20 a month" line parents already know from Roblox Premium.
- Yearly prices are **under $50 and under $100**, which are easy one-time "birthday or holiday gift" decisions for parents.
- **Don't lock YouTube posting behind the top plan.** Teens want to post their videos. Only "making animations for paying clients" belongs to Studio.

### Extra AI credit packs
For paid plans. Packs stay valid for 12 months; monthly plan credits reset each month.

| Pack | Price | Per 100 credits | Our AI cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 500 credits | **$4.99** | ~$1.00 | $1.25 |
| 1,200 credits | **$9.99** | ~$0.83 | $3.00 |
| 3,500 credits | **$24.99** | ~$0.71 | $8.75 |

- **Smallest pack is $4.99** because of the 30¢ card fee.
- These packs are much cheaper than Pika (~$2.67 per 100) and Powtoon (~$33 per 100).
- **Only the person who pays** (usually the parent) can buy packs. No one-tap impulse buys for kids.

### 14-day free trial
- **Creator-level features, 150 AI credits** (costs us at most ~38¢ per trial).
- **No card needed**, because many teens don't have one. At the end, the kid taps a **"Ask a parent"** button that sends the parent a checkout link.
- Expect fewer trials to turn into paid users than with card-required trials, but 3–5× more people trying it. There will also be far fewer angry-parent chargebacks.
- Optional: exports during the trial carry a small "Made with Diamond Animator" mark (free advertising). Remove it on any paid plan.
- **Suggestion (Arthur/dad decide):** when the trial ends, **don't lock kids out of their work.** Drop them into a "free mode": open and view projects, draw frame by frame, export with a watermark, ~20 AI credits a month (≈5¢ of cost). Almost every competitor has a free tier, and it keeps people coming back to upgrade later. This isn't a 4th plan for sale, just what happens after the trial.

### Beta offer: first 50 users, 40% off the first year
| | Starter | Creator | Studio |
|---|---|---|---|
| Yearly, beta price for year 1 | **$29.40** (was $49) | **$59.40** (was $99) | **$101.40** (was $169) |
| Monthly, beta price for 12 months | $3.59 | $7.19 | $11.99 |

- **How to set it up:**
  - A Stripe coupon, 40% off: "once" on yearly plans, "repeating 12 months" on monthly plans.
  - Count a spot only when someone **actually pays**, and show the real count on the "spots left" bar.
  - **Push the yearly version.** It brings in cash up front and keeps beta users for a whole year of feedback.
- **Be clear about renewal:** say exactly what it renews at (e.g. "then $99/yr"). Email a **reminder 7 days before renewal** and make cancelling one click. That's good for trust, and some US states require it for auto-renewing plans.
- **Optional, cheap bonus:** **double AI credits during the beta.** It costs at most $0.75–$7.50 per user per month but feels generous. Also a "Founding Animator" badge.

### Price points that should convert well for teens (with parents paying)
- Monthly prices **under $10** for the entry plan. Yearly prices **under $50 and under $100**. Use .99 on monthly prices and round numbers on yearly prices.
- **Show yearly first** with "save ~30%", and show monthly too. Mark the middle plan "Most popular".
- Make it easy for parents: an "Ask a parent" checkout link, **gift codes** for birthdays and holidays, no card for the trial, renewal reminders, and a clear refund policy.
- **Kids' privacy:** users under 13 need verifiable parental consent under the updated COPPA rule (compliance date 2026-04-22). A parent's card payment is one accepted way to get that consent. Have the lawyer check the sign-up flow (this is already on the website spec's legal list).
- **Sales tax/VAT:** if we sell outside the US, Stripe Tax (+0.5%) or a "merchant of record" service handles it. Decide before launch.
